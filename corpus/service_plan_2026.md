# Service Improvement Plan 2026

Goal: cut the average repair wait time from 9 days to 3 days by March 2027.

Actions:
- Grow the network from 38 to 60 service centres by March 2027, with most new centres in Tier 2 cities.
- Hire 120 people in 2026, of whom 80 will be service technicians.
- Launch a mobile repair van service in Pune and Bengaluru for Cargo business customers.
- Stock spare motors at every centre so motor swaps no longer wait for parts from Chakan.
