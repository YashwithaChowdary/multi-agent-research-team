# Lumora Bikes: Company Overview

Lumora Bikes is an electric bicycle maker founded in 2019 in Pune, India, by Meera Joshi and Arvind Rao. The company designs its frames and software in Pune and assembles bikes at a plant in Chakan.

Lumora had 340 employees at the end of March 2026. It sells three product lines: the Lumora City commuter bike, the Lumora Trail mountain e-bike and the Lumora Cargo utility bike.

Bikes are sold online and through 38 company-run service centres across India, which also handle repairs and warranty claims. The Lumora app connects to every bike over Bluetooth for ride data, theft alerts and firmware updates.
