# Battery Cell Supply

Lumora buys lithium-ion cells from two sources. Arka Cells in Chennai supplies 70% of cells. The remaining 30% are imported from South Korea.

The company plans to source 90% of cells within India by 2027, mainly by expanding the contract with Arka Cells. Imports expose Lumora to currency changes and shipping delays; a two-month shipping delay in 2024 stopped Trail production for three weeks.

The average cell price paid by Lumora fell 14% during 2025.
