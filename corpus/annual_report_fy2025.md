# Lumora Bikes Annual Report FY2025 (April 2024 to March 2025)

Revenue for FY2025 was 412 crore rupees, up 38% from FY2024. Lumora sold 61,000 bikes in the year. The Lumora City made up 58% of units sold.

The Lumora Cargo was launched in March 2025, so it contributed very little to FY2025 revenue.

Gross margin was 24%. The company recorded a net loss of 23 crore rupees, mainly because of warranty costs on the Trail model and spending on new service centres.

Management expects the Cargo and lower battery cell prices to improve margins in FY2026.
