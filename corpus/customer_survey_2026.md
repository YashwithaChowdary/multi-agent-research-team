# Customer Survey 2026 (2,300 responses, January 2026)

Lumora's Net Promoter Score was 46.

Top complaints:
1. Service wait times: the average wait for a repair was 9 days.
2. App connectivity: Bluetooth disconnections, mostly on the City.
3. Price of spare batteries.

64% of Cargo buyers are small businesses such as grocery delivery, couriers and caterers. Cargo owners gave the highest satisfaction score of the three models.
