# Investor Update: Q2 FY2026 (July to September 2025)

Q2 FY2026 revenue was 131 crore rupees. The Lumora Cargo now provides 21% of revenue, ahead of plan.

Correction to FY2025 figures: units sold in FY2025 have been restated from 61,000 to 59,400 because returned bikes are now subtracted. Revenue for FY2025 is unchanged at 412 crore rupees.

International: Lumora will begin a pilot in the Netherlands, starting in Utrecht in September 2026, with 400 Cargo bikes leased to delivery businesses. No other countries are planned before 2027.
