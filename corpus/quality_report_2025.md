# Quality Report 2025

Return rates within 12 months of sale, calendar year 2025:
- Lumora City: 2.1%
- Lumora Trail: 4.8%
- Lumora Cargo: 3.2% (based on nine months of sales)

The Trail had the highest return rate. Water getting into the rear hub motor caused 61% of Trail returns. Most of these bikes were built between August 2024 and January 2025.

For the City, the most common return reason was app connectivity problems after a firmware update in June 2025. For the Cargo, it was brake pad wear under heavy loads.
