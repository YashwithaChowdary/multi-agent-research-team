# Postmortem: Trail Rear Hub Motor Water Ingress

Summary: In August 2024 Lumora changed the supplier of the motor shaft seal from Kavach Seals to RubberTech to cut costs. The new seal did not hold the IP67 rating under pressure washing; tests later showed the motors behaved closer to IP54.

Impact: water entered the rear hub motor on Trail bikes, causing motor failures and the high Trail return rate. Warranty repairs and replacements cost 6.2 crore rupees.

Fix: Lumora switched back to Kavach Seals in February 2025 and added a pressure-wash test for every 50th motor at the Chakan plant. Affected owners were offered a free seal replacement.

Lesson: supplier changes for sealing parts now require a full IP67 test before production.
